Privacy Policy / Политика конфиденциальности

AndryushaExplorer, 18.11.2026

AndryushaExplorer requests access to the user's Google Drive via Google OAuth solely to browse, upload, download, copy, move and delete files at the user's request.

The app runs on the user's computer. Access tokens are stored locally on that computer. Files travel directly between the user's computer and Google. The developer does not receive, store or process users' files, tokens or personal data on any server.

Data is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
